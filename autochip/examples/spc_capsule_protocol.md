# Semi-permeable capsule (SPC) workflow: generic protocol

Paraphrased from published SPC methods (Leonaviciene & Mazutis, Nucleic Acids Research 2023;
Baronas et al., Science 2026). All values are placeholders for illustration, not a validated recipe.

## Cell preparation
1. Culture adherent cells in a culture vessel at 37 °C with 5% CO2 until confluent (placeholder: 3 days, medium change daily).
2. Remove the medium and rinse the cells once with buffer (200 µL).
3. Add dissociation enzyme (100 µL) and incubate 5 min at 37 °C to detach the cells.
4. Add quench medium (200 µL) and mix gently to stop the enzyme.
5. Centrifuge 300 g for 5 min, remove the supernatant.
6. Resuspend the pellet in wash buffer (500 µL); repeat the wash once.
7. Pass the suspension through a 40 µm cell strainer.
8. Count cells and check viability.
9. Resuspend cells in core solution at the loading density (placeholder: 2 x 10^6 cells/mL).

## Capsule generation
10. Co-flow the cell/core solution with the shell polymer solution (kept warm) and focus with fluorinated oil
    containing surfactant in a flow-focusing junction to make droplets (~70 µm). Placeholder flows: core 2 µL/min,
    shell 2 µL/min, oil 10 µL/min.
11. Collect the emulsion and keep it on ice for 15–30 min so the shell gels.
12. Break the emulsion: add emulsion-breaking agent and remove the oil phase.
13. Photocure the capsule shells with 405 nm light (placeholder: 30 s) in the presence of photoinitiator.
14. Wash the capsules three times in buffer to remove oil and free reagents.
15. Filter the capsules through a 100 µm strainer to remove aggregates.

## Processing inside capsules
16. Exchange the buffer for lysis buffer and incubate (placeholder time and temperature).
17. Wash the capsules three times in wash buffer.
18. Exchange into reverse transcription mix and incubate (placeholder time and temperature).
19. Wash the capsules twice.
20. Split the capsules into four equal portions.
21. Run index PCR on each portion with a different index primer mix (placeholder number of cycles).
22. Release the cDNA by digesting the capsule shells with a shell-digesting enzyme (placeholder time and temperature).
23. Bind the library to magnetic beads, wash twice with ethanol wash solution on a magnet, and elute in water.
24. Measure library concentration and fragment size.
