Grow three iPSC-derived cell types (neurons, microglia, astrocytes) side by side, first apart and then
sharing medium. Then detach the cells, wrap single cells in semi-permeable hydrogel capsules, break the
emulsion and cure the capsule shells with light, lyse the cells inside the capsules, wash, and reverse
transcribe the RNA. Split the capsules into four sublibraries for index PCR, release the cDNA from the
capsules and clean the library up with magnetic beads so it is ready for sequencing.
