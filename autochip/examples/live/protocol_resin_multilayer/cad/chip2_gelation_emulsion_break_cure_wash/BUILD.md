# Build chip2_gelation_emulsion_break_cure_wash by hand in CAD

Gelation, emulsion breaking, curing and capsule wash chip. Generated by autochip from `chips/chip2_gelation_emulsion_break_cure_wash.json` (constraints `resin_print_multilayer`, resin_print). **Placeholder geometry**: a first automatic layout; check it before you make it.

Units are mm. Origin at the bottom-left corner of the chip, x to the right, y up, z up out of the chip. The top view `chip2_gelation_emulsion_break_cure_wash.svg` / `.png` shows everything below to scale, with a 1 mm grid.

## 1. Board

1. Draw a 57 x 43.5 mm rectangle with its bottom-left corner at (0, 0).
2. Layer stack, bottom to top (total 2.5 mm):
   - `base`: resin, 1 mm (z 0 to 1) - resin_print (clear resin)
   - `fluidic`: resin, 0.5 mm (z 1 to 1.5) - fluidic layer
   - `lid`: resin, 1 mm (z 1.5 to 2.5) - sealed by printed_closed
3. All fluidic features sit in layer `fluidic`, cut down from z = 1.5 mm. Channels are 0.3 mm deep; chambers as listed below.

## 2. Modules (chambers), in flow order

1. **gelation_delay** - 31.8 x 34.5 mm, 0.3 mm deep, bottom-left corner at (4.700, 4.500), centre (20.6015, 21.75); 150 uL; type mixer. Draw it as a mixer (delay_line, parametric).
   - Does (steps 11): Gives the emulsion a 30 min cold residence on the cold plate so the shells gel. A low flow keeps the line small enough for the chip.
   - Inside: a serpentine of 333.3 mm of 1.5 mm channel, 11 lanes filling the box.
2. **capsule_trap** - 14.1 x 7.07 mm, 0.5 mm deep, bottom-left corner at (37.700, 18.214), centre (44.7711, 21.75); 50 uL; type cell_wash. Draw it as a cell_wash (trap_chamber, parametric).
   - Does (steps 12, 13, 14): A 25 um mesh holds the capsules while the breaking agent removes the oil, the photoinitiator is flushed in and 405 nm light cures the shells, and buffer washes them.
   - Floor: a 25 um mesh (bought sheet, inserted at assembly).

## 5. Make and seal

Print the chip closed (channels inside the part); drain and flush uncured resin out through the ports.

Zone `cold_zone_area`: gelation_delay, capsule_trap held at 4 C by off-chip equipment (covers gelation_delay, capsule_trap).
