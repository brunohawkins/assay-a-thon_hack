# Build chip3_filter_lysis_rt_wash by hand in CAD

Capsule filter, lysis, reverse transcription and wash chip. Generated by autochip from `chips/chip3_filter_lysis_rt_wash.json` (constraints `resin_print_multilayer`, resin_print). **Placeholder geometry**: a first automatic layout; check it before you make it.

Units are mm. Origin at the bottom-left corner of the chip, x to the right, y up, z up out of the chip. The top view `chip3_filter_lysis_rt_wash.svg` / `.png` shows everything below to scale, with a 1 mm grid.

## 1. Board

1. Draw a 30 x 19 mm rectangle with its bottom-left corner at (0, 0).
2. Layer stack, bottom to top (total 2.5 mm):
   - `base`: resin, 1 mm (z 0 to 1) - resin_print (clear resin)
   - `fluidic`: resin, 0.5 mm (z 1 to 1.5) - fluidic layer
   - `lid`: resin, 1 mm (z 1.5 to 2.5) - sealed by printed_closed
3. All fluidic features sit in layer `fluidic`, cut down from z = 1.5 mm. Channels are 0.3 mm deep; chambers as listed below.

## 2. Modules (chambers), in flow order

1. **aggregate_filter** - 5 x 10 mm, 0.3 mm deep, bottom-left corner at (4.700, 4.500), centre (7.2, 9.5); 7.5 uL; type filter. Draw it as a filter (pillar_filter, parametric).
   - Does (steps 15): A pillar filter with 100 um gaps removes aggregates from the 70 um capsules.
   - Inside: a row of pillars with 100 um gaps across the flow (capsule_aggregates).
2. **reaction_trap** - 14.1 x 7.07 mm, 0.5 mm deep, bottom-left corner at (10.900, 5.964), centre (17.9711, 9.5); 50 uL; type cell_wash. Draw it as a cell_wash (trap_chamber, parametric).
   - Does (steps 16, 17, 18, 19): Holds the capsules on a 25 um mesh while lysis buffer, wash buffer, RT mix and wash buffer are flushed in turn. All are water-based, so one shared reagent_line is used and the syringe is swapped between steps.
   - Floor: a 25 um mesh (bought sheet, inserted at assembly).

## 3. Channels

Each channel is a path of straight segments through the listed points (its centreline), drawn at its width and cut to its depth. It starts at the edge of the `from` part and ends at the edge of the `to` part; at a port it runs through the port centre on to the far edge of the hole.

1. **ch_inlet_1_aggregate_filter**: inlet_1 -> aggregate_filter, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries capsules, 2.70 mm long. Path: (2, 9.5) -> (2.5, 9.5) -> (4.7, 9.5).
2. **ch_aggregate_filter_reaction_trap**: aggregate_filter -> reaction_trap, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries capsules, 1.20 mm long. Path: (9.7, 9.5) -> (10.9, 9.5).
3. **ch_reaction_trap_outlet_1**: reaction_trap -> outlet_1, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries capsules, 2.96 mm long. Path: (25.0421, 9.5) -> (27.5, 9.5) -> (28, 9.5).
4. **ch_reagent_line_in_reaction_trap**: reagent_line_in -> reaction_trap, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries liquid, 3.96 mm long. Path: (17.97, 17) -> (17.97, 16.5) -> (17.97, 13.0355).
5. **ch_reaction_trap_reaction_trap_waste_out**: reaction_trap -> reaction_trap_waste_out, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries liquid, 3.96 mm long. Path: (17.97, 5.9645) -> (17.97, 2.5) -> (17.97, 2).

## 4. Ports

Each port is a round hole from the top face down into the fluidic layer, centred on its channel, which runs across the whole hole.

- **inlet_1** (inlet, load): 1 mm hole at (2.5, 9.5), from z = 2.5 down to z = 1. main inlet: inlet_1 from chip2_gelation_emulsion_break_cure_wash.outlet_1.
- **outlet_1** (outlet, harvest): 1 mm hole at (27.5, 9.5), from z = 2.5 down to z = 1. main outlet -> external.
- **reagent_line_in** (inlet, reagent): 1 mm hole at (17.97, 16.5), from z = 2.5 down to z = 1. reagent 'reagent_line' into reaction_trap.
- **reaction_trap_waste_out** (outlet, waste): 1 mm hole at (17.97, 2.5), from z = 2.5 down to z = 1. 'reaction_trap_waste' out of reaction_trap.

## 5. Make and seal

Print the chip closed (channels inside the part); drain and flush uncured resin out through the ports.

## 6. Constraint findings

- **WARNING** (min_feature.gap_mm): chamber 'reaction_trap' needs a 25 um mesh, finer than the 100 um this process can make: it has to be a bought mesh sheet inserted during assembly.
