Units are mm. Origin at the bottom-left corner of the chip, x to the right, y up, z up out of the chip. The top view `chip4_shell_release_bead_capture_wash_elution.svg` / `.png` shows everything below to scale, with a 1 mm grid.

## 1. Board

1. Draw a 39 x 16 mm rectangle with its bottom-left corner at (0, 0).
2. Layer stack, bottom to top (total 2.5 mm):
   - `base`: resin, 1 mm (z 0 to 1) - resin_print (clear resin)
   - `fluidic`: resin, 0.5 mm (z 1 to 1.5) - fluidic layer
   - `lid`: resin, 1 mm (z 1.5 to 2.5) - sealed by printed_closed
3. All fluidic features sit in layer `fluidic`, cut down from z = 1.5 mm. Channels are 0.3 mm deep; chambers as listed below.
