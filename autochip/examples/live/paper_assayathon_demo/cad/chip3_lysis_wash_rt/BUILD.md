# Build chip3_lysis_wash_rt by hand in CAD

Lysis, wash and reverse transcription chip. Generated by autochip from `chips/chip3_lysis_wash_rt.json` (constraints `assayathon_demo`, resin_print_hot_emboss). **Placeholder geometry**: a first automatic layout; check it before you make it.

Units are mm. Origin at the bottom-left corner of the chip, x to the right, y up, z up out of the chip. The top view `chip3_lysis_wash_rt.svg` / `.png` shows everything below to scale, with a 1 mm grid.

## 1. Board

1. Draw a 26.5 x 15.5 mm rectangle with its bottom-left corner at (0, 0).
2. Layer stack, bottom to top (total 1.9 mm):
   - `substrate`: resin, 1.5 mm (z 0 to 1.5) - printed / embossed body (clear resin); the fluidic relief is embossed into its top face
   - `fluidic`: resin, 0.3 mm (z 1.5 to 1.8) - embossed channels and chambers, one depth
   - `seal`: sealing film, 0.1 mm (z 1.8 to 1.9) - sealed by film
3. All fluidic features sit in layer `fluidic`, cut down from z = 1.8 mm. Channels are 0.3 mm deep; chambers as listed below.

## 2. Modules (chambers), in flow order

1. **lysis_rt_trap** - 18.3 x 9.13 mm, 0.3 mm deep, bottom-left corner at (4.000, 3.186), centre (13.1287, 7.75); 50 uL; type cell_wash. Draw it as a cell_wash (trap_chamber, parametric).
   - Does (steps 16, 17, 18, 19): A mesh with 25 um pores holds the capsules while lysis buffer, wash buffer, RT mix and wash buffer are flushed through in turn. They share one water-based reagent_line, with the syringe swapped between steps. The chip sits on a heater for the incubations.
   - Floor: a 25 um mesh (bought sheet, inserted at assembly).

## 3. Channels

Each channel is a path of straight segments through the listed points (its centreline), drawn at its width and cut to its depth. It starts at the edge of the `from` part and ends at the edge of the `to` part; at a port it runs through the port centre on to the far edge of the hole.

1. **ch_inlet_1_lysis_rt_trap**: inlet_1 -> lysis_rt_trap, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries capsules, 3.00 mm long. Path: (1, 7.75) -> (1.5, 7.75) -> (4, 7.75).
2. **ch_lysis_rt_trap_outlet_1**: lysis_rt_trap -> outlet_1, 0.5 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries capsules, 3.24 mm long. Path: (22.2574, 7.75) -> (25, 7.75) -> (25.5, 7.75).
3. **ch_reagent_line_in_lysis_rt_trap**: reagent_line_in -> lysis_rt_trap, 0.25 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries liquid, 2.19 mm long. Path: (13.13, 14.5) -> (13.13, 14) -> (13.13, 12.3144).
4. **ch_lysis_rt_trap_trap_waste_out**: lysis_rt_trap -> trap_waste_out, 0.25 mm wide, 0.3 mm deep, carries liquid, 2.19 mm long. Path: (13.13, 3.1856) -> (13.13, 1.5) -> (13.13, 1).

## 4. Ports

Each port is a round hole from the top face down into the fluidic layer, centred on its channel, which runs across the whole hole.

- **inlet_1** (inlet, load): 1 mm hole at (1.5, 7.75), from z = 1.9 down to z = 1.5. main inlet: inlet_1 from chip2_emulsion_break_curing_wash_filter.outlet_1.
- **outlet_1** (outlet, harvest): 1 mm hole at (25, 7.75), from z = 1.9 down to z = 1.5. main outlet -> external.
- **reagent_line_in** (inlet, reagent): 1 mm hole at (13.13, 14), from z = 1.9 down to z = 1.5. reagent 'reagent_line' into lysis_rt_trap.
- **trap_waste_out** (outlet, waste): 1 mm hole at (13.13, 1.5), from z = 1.9 down to z = 1.5. 'trap_waste' out of lysis_rt_trap.

## 5. Make and seal

Print (or emboss from a printed master) the substrate with the fluidic relief facing up, all features 0.3 mm deep. Punch/drill the port holes through the film (or the substrate), then seal the open channels with the film.

## 6. Constraint findings

- **ERROR** (min_feature.gap_mm): chamber 'lysis_rt_trap' retains with pillars 25 um apart, below the 60 um smallest gap this process makes. This process has one fluidic layer, so a bought mesh floor has nothing to clamp it either. Needs a bought mesh held by a second layer (another process), finer features, or the retention step off-chip.
