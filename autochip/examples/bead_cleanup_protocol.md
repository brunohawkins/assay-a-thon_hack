# Magnetic bead DNA clean-up (generic, after any amplification)

Generic solid-phase reversible immobilisation clean-up as described in many published methods.
Values are typical placeholders.

1. Mix the DNA sample (50 µL) with bead suspension (40 µL, 0.8x) and incubate 5 min at room temperature.
2. Place on a magnet for 2 min until the liquid is clear; remove and discard the supernatant.
3. Wash the beads with ethanol wash solution (200 µL) on the magnet for 30 s; discard. Repeat once (2 washes in total).
4. Air-dry the beads for 2 min.
5. Remove from the magnet, resuspend the beads in elution buffer (20 µL), incubate 2 min.
6. Place on the magnet and collect the clear eluate containing the clean DNA.
7. Measure DNA concentration.
